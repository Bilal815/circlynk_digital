Most software projects fail before they start — not because of bad code, but because nobody agreed on what the software was actually supposed to do.

CircLynk Digital is a lean, founder-led technology firm based in Karachi, Pakistan. We serve Pakistani SMEs who need software that fits their reality — PKR pricing, WhatsApp support, Urdu where needed — and US startups who need offshore delivery they can actually trust.

## What we actually do

We build custom software, automation tools, and digital products. From free browser-based tools like our Python compiler to full enterprise transformation projects. Everything starts with your problem — not technology.

## Why we exist

Pakistani software talent is genuinely world-class. The gap isn't capability — it's trust, communication, and delivery discipline. CircLynk bridges that gap by running every project with the accountability standards US clients expect and the local knowledge Pakistani clients need.

## Based in Karachi. Working globally.

Pakistani clients: PKR pricing, WhatsApp-first, Urdu where it helps.
US clients: EST timezone overlap, email and Slack, USD pricing, Payoneer.
