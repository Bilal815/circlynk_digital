CircLynk Digital ("we", "us", "our") operates circlynkdigital.com. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email, and message when you use our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter.

We also collect standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited through Google Analytics (anonymized).

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to respond to enquiries, send newsletters you've opted into, and improve our website.

## Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for 12 months. Newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe.

## Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.
